# Product Photos

Place your product photos in this folder. The images should be named:

- `egg-fidget.jpg` - 3D printed egg fidget
- `samurai-sword.jpg` - 3D printed Samurai Sword
- `articulated-dragon.jpg` - Articulated dragon
- `shiny-dragon.jpg` - Shiny dragon
- `skull-pass-through.jpg` - Skull pass-through toy

## How to add photos:

1. Take photos of your 3D-printed items
2. Name them exactly as listed above
3. Place them in this folder: `public/Photos/`
4. The website will automatically display them

## Image recommendations:

- **Format**: JPG or PNG
- **Size**: 800x600px or larger (will be automatically resized)
- **File size**: Keep under 500KB for fast loading
